The Raasta Mission

Empowering Smart Travel in Belagavi

Raasta was designed to address daily traffic frustrations caused by sudden unnotified road digging, railway crossing work, and monsoonal waterlogging in Belagavi, Karnataka.

The Core Problem

Commuters often embark on their regular trips only to encounter an unexpected bridge barrier at Congress Road or single-lane water pipeline digging on Khanapur Road. Conventional navigation apps often report generalized traffic delay without explaining *why* the road is blocked or whether two-wheelers can navigate the diversion.

The Raasta Solution

Raasta marries official municipal advisories (Belagavi City Corporation, Traffic Police) with crowdsourced confirmation. A transparent confidence score prevents fake rumours, while route-level alerts notify drivers before they step into the car.
